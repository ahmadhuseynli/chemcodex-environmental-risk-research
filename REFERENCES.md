# References and connecting sources

This page lists the main public sources that frame the current research architecture. It is not a substitute for checking the exact current rule that applies in a particular jurisdiction.

## Offshore chemical control

- OSPAR - Offshore Chemicals  
  https://www.ospar.org/work-areas/oic/chemicals

- OSPAR Decision 2000/2 - Harmonised Mandatory Control System for the Use and Discharge of Offshore Chemicals  
  https://www.ospar.org/convention/agreements

- OSPAR Recommendation 2010/03 - Harmonised Offshore Chemical Notification Format (HOCNF)  
  https://www.ospar.org/documents?v=51284

- OSPAR Agreement 2012-05e - Guidelines for Completing HOCNF  
  The recovered scientific audit notes a 2025 update to the HOCNF guidance.  
  https://www.ospar.org/convention/agreements/page6?t=32281

- OSPAR Agreement 2013-06 - PLONOR list  
  The recovered audit notes the list was updated in 2026. PLONOR status is treated in ChemCodex as a routing/context flag rather than universal proof of zero scenario-specific risk.  
  https://www.ospar.org/convention/agreements/page9

## Environmental effect and exposure assessment

- ECHA - Guidance on Information Requirements and Chemical Safety Assessment  
  https://echa.europa.eu/guidance-documents/guidance-on-information-requirements-and-chemical-safety-assessment

  Relevant chapters:
  - **R.10** - environmental dose/concentration-response and PNEC derivation;
  - **R.16** - environmental exposure assessment.

- EUR-Lex - REACH Regulation and Annex XIII PBT/vPvB criteria  
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/2023-06-29/eng

  Relevant bioaccumulation criteria used in the technical audit:
  - **B: BCF > 2,000**
  - **vB: BCF > 5,000**

- ECHA - Guidance on the Application of the CLP Criteria  
  https://echa.europa.eu/guidance-documents/guidance-on-clp

  Relevant to aquatic classification and the use of substance-specific M-factors in mixture summation.

## GESAMP

- GESAMP Reports and Studies No. 102 - Hazard Evaluation Procedure for Chemicals Carried by Ships (2019 procedure; 2020 publication)  
  https://www.gesamp.org/publications/gesamp-hazard-evaluation-procedure-for-chemicals-carried-by-ships-2019

The project used GESAMP-like aquatic hazard bands historically, but internal ChemCodex scores are not presented as equivalent to the full GESAMP Hazard Evaluation Procedure.
