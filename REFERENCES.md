# References and connecting sources

This page lists the main public sources that frame the current research architecture. It is not a substitute for checking the exact current rule that applies in a particular jurisdiction.

## Offshore chemical control

- OSPAR - Offshore Chemicals  
  https://www.ospar.org/work-areas/oic/chemicals

- OSPAR Decision 2000/2 - Harmonised Mandatory Control System for the Use and Discharge of Offshore Chemicals  
  https://www.ospar.org/documents?v=51286

- OSPAR Recommendation 2010/03 - Harmonised Offshore Chemical Notification Format (HOCNF)  
  https://www.ospar.org/convention/agreements/page11

- OSPAR PLONOR list - substances used/discharged offshore considered to pose little or no risk to the environment  
  https://www.ospar.org/convention/agreements/page9

## Environmental effect and exposure assessment

- ECHA - Guidance on Information Requirements and Chemical Safety Assessment  
  https://echa.europa.eu/guidance-documents/guidance-on-information-requirements-and-chemical-safety-assessment

  Relevant chapters include R.10 for environmental dose/concentration-response and R.16 for environmental exposure assessment.

- EUR-Lex - REACH Regulation and Annex XIII PBT/vPvB criteria  
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2006/1907/oj

- ECHA - Guidance on CLP  
  https://echa.europa.eu/guidance-documents/guidance-on-clp
